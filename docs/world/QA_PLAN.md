# Football World QA Plan

Validate against synchronized professional and local football footage.

## Pitch and ball
- 105 × 68 m markings and 7.32 × 2.44 m goals.
- Roll deceleration by surface, moisture and grass length.
- Bounce, spin and wind response by ball profile.
- Goal-net recoil without explosive deformation.
- Wear concentrated in goalmouths, central traffic and slide zones.

## Stadium and crowd
- Camera visibility for every venue archetype.
- Attendance never exceeds capacity and is not always sold out.
- Away supporters remain sectioned and celebrate only after confirmed events.
- Staff remain outside gameplay space.
- Screens show authoritative score and clock.
- Crowd density and staff count do not introduce input latency.

## Tournament and ceremony
- Round-robin fixture completeness and home/away reversal.
- Configurable tiebreaker ordering.
- Seed and protected-group draw constraints.
- Ceremony length remains skippable.
- Trophy appears only in correct match contexts.

## Cultural review
- Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya and Somali usage follows venue context.
- Ethiopian regional identity is specific and respectful, not generic.
- Flags, national symbols and anthem slots require verified source assets before official use.
