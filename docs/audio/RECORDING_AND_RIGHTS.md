# Recording, Localization and Rights Standard

- Capture masters at 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV with clean gain staging and no clipping.
- Record isolated individuals, small groups, medium groups and large groups at close and distant perspectives.
- Capture loopable stadium ambiences and impulse responses where permitted.
- Store language, speaker, performer, microphone, location and take metadata.
- Obtain performer/model releases and venue permissions where required.
- Do not extract audio from broadcasts, commercial songs or other games.
- Do not include unauthorized club songs, anthem recordings or supporter recordings.
- Use original, commissioned, public-domain or fully licensed sources only.
- Update `data/audio/licensing-register.json` with creator, performer, license, territory, expiration, platforms, commercial permission, attribution, source and signed-release status.

The repository intentionally ships procedural legal placeholders instead of pretending final recorded assets exist.
